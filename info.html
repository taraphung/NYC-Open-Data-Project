This is the information page. It contains all the data cards and two filtering options. The first one is on filtering by the tree's status and name. The other one is on the tree's zipcode and damage on the sidewalk. Both have a text box and a drop down. In the text box, put in the status/zipcode you want to filter by. You can copy and paste these values from the cards, as shown on the right. Then, click the drop down menu and choose the value you want.
