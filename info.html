NYC Open Data

This is the information page. It contains 1,000 data cards and two filtering options.

The first filtering option is by the tree's status and name. Click on the text box and type "Alive" for the status. Then, click on the drop down menu to select the tree you want to filter by. After clicking "Search", the results will show on the right.

The second filtering option is by the tree's zipcode and damage on the sidewalk. Click on the text box and put in the zipcode you want to filter by. You can copy and paste the zipcode value from the cards, shown on the right. Then, click the drop down menu and choose the value you want. Lastly, click "Search" to see the results.
