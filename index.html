Tree Census Home Page

➼ This website focuses on the data of different types of trees in New York. In the following pages, you will be able to see information cards on different trees and an analysis on them. Each card includes the following information: Tree Name (Type), Zipcode, Address, Status, Damage on Sidewalk, Latitude, and Longitude.

➼ In the "Info Cards" web page, there will be 1,000 information cards on different trees in New York. With this data, you can filter/search by the tree's status and name and by the zipcode and damage caused.

➼ In the "Analysis" web page, you will be able to analyze the information through the latitude and longitude using the map. You will also be able to see the percentages of different types of trees using a bar chart and pie chart.

➼ Open Data is free public data published by New York City agencies and other partners. NYC Open Data is managed by the Open Data Team at the NYC Office of Technology and Innovation (OTI). The team works with City agencies to identify and make data available, coordinate platform operations and improvements, and promote the use of Open Data both within government and throughout NYC.

➼ Many different people use Open Data. This includes developers, researches, journalists, city agencies, nonprofits, and many others.

➼ In the NYC Open Data website, you can search for different data sets. Some commonly searched ones include 311 Service Requests, Motor Vehicle Collisions, Surveys, and Tree Censuses, which you will be analyzing in this website project.
