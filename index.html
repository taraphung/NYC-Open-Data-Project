Tree Census Home Page

➼ This website focuses on the data of different types of trees in New York. In the following pages, you will be able to see information cards on different trees and an analysis on them. Each card includes the following information: Tree Name (Type), Zipcode, Address, Status, Damage on Sidewalk, Latitude, and Longitude.

➼ In the "Info Cards" tab, there will be 1,000 information cards on different trees in New York. With this data, you can filter/search by the tree's status and name and by the zipcode and damage caused.

➼ In the "Analysis" tab, you will be able to analyze the information through the latitude and longitude using the map. You will also be able to see the percentages of different types of trees using a bar chart and pie chart.

➼ Open Data is free public data published by New York City agencies and other partners. Here, New York City agencies and other organizations publish their government-produced data.
